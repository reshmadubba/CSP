QR-Based Local Information System

Access important local information quickly and easily using a single platform. Find nearby hospitals, police stations, emergency services, transportation, government offices and local announcements.

About Our System

The QR-Based Local Information System provides users with easy access to essential information about their locality. The system is designed to help people find useful services without searching through multiple websites or applications.


For our project, Vizianagaram district area is used as the initial location reference. The system can be expanded to other locations in the future.

Our Services

🏥 Hospitals

Find information about nearby hospitals, their locations, available services and directions.

👮 Police Stations

Find nearby police stations and useful information about their locations and services.

🚑 Emergency Services

Quickly access important emergency services and emergency contact options.

🚌 Public Transport

Find useful information about public transport, nearby transport facilities and locations.

🏛️ Government Offices

Access information about nearby government offices and important public services.

📢 Announcements

View important local announcements, notices and updates in one place.

How It Works

Scan the QR code provided at the designated location to open the Local Information System. Select the service you need and access the relevant information quickly.
